## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A shift-handover console for one scheme: every number on screen
declares its own status (live / stale / unavailable / refused), so the model's
limits are furniture rather than footnotes. Refuses the card-grid-of-equal-weight
dashboard where nothing outranks anything.

**OWN-WORLD:** Pinned by the user (`design_inspo/`): light ground, rounded cards,
persistent left rail, Santander red + bike-frame navy, charts in red/navy/grey,
never a rainbow. Recognisable with content removed by the status chip on every
card and the fixed instrument column of tabular figures down each table's right.

**STORY:** A planner sees the network's shape, trusts it because every caveat is
on screen, and leaves with a day's number.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Left rail (mark, two sections, dataset span). Right: one
title row with the live/stale chip, then a four-tile status strip, then one large
figure owning the remaining height — never a grid of equals.

**FORM:** Control room; candidate 5 of 7; seed 5ceb036d.

**Raise — grid discipline (from split-flap concourse, competitive):** forecast
tables are departure boards — fixed columns, rows as live entities, values step
on change.

**Raise — snap, don't glide (from depot blind, competitive):** state changes step
and settle; narrow screens drop columns, never shrink type.

**Raise — one control, whole sheet (from Miura fold, declined):** every filter
visibly propagates to every figure at once.

**Raise — annotate in place (from tensegrity, declined):** the temperature block
is bracketed and labelled inside the heatmap, not captioned beneath it.

**Raise — one thing owns the frame (from vertical feed, declined):** the primary
figure commits a whole region rather than sharing a wall of equal cards.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
